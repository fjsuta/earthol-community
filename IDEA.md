
# 💡 项目创意说明

## 🌍 创意来源

这个项目的灵感来自于一个非常简单但深刻的想法：**如果现实世界是一个多人在线游戏，那会是什么样子？**

### 🎮 游戏化的现实视角

- **46亿年开服** - 地球已经运行了46亿年，这是一个极其宏大的世界观
- **无官方主线任务** - 人生没有标准答案，每个人都可以走出自己的路
- **玩家自主探索** - 所有知识、智慧都需要主动去发现
- **华夏服的独特性** - 唯一从未断代的古文明，这是一个非常有吸引力的设定

### 🤖 AI设计理念

传统的AI助手通常有预设脚本，主动提供信息。但我们设计的AI是：
- **被动应答** - 玩家不问，AI不说
- **思维链推理** - 展示思考过程，而不是直接给出答案
- **记忆功能** - 记住和玩家的对话历史
- **本地化部署** - 支持本地Ollama，保护隐私

## 🎯 核心价值观

### 1. 探索精神
鼓励玩家主动去发现、去探索，而不是被动接受信息灌输。

### 2. 文明传承
强调华夏文明的连续性，同时尊重各地区的文化差异。

### 3. 开放自由
完全开源，允许自由二次开发和商业使用。

### 4. 隐私优先
支持本地AI部署，数据存储在用户自己的服务器上。

# 💡 Project Idea

## 🌍 Inspiration Source

The inspiration for this project comes from a very simple yet profound question: **What if the real world was a massively multiplayer online game?**

### 🎮 Gaming the Real World Perspective

- **4.6 billion years server** - Earth has been running for 4.6 billion years, that's an immense worldview
- **No official main quest** - Life has no standard answer, everyone can forge their own path
- **Player-driven exploration** - All knowledge and wisdom must be actively discovered
- **Huaxia Server uniqueness** - The only continuous ancient civilization, a very compelling setting

### 🤖 AI Design Philosophy

Traditional AI assistants usually have preset scripts and actively provide information. But our AI is designed to be:
- **Passive responder** - AI only speaks when asked
- **Chain-of-thought reasoning** - Shows the thinking process instead of direct answers
- **Memory feature** - Remembers conversation history with players
- **Local deployment** - Supports local Ollama for privacy

## 🎯 Core Values

### 1. Spirit of Exploration
Encourages players to actively discover and explore instead of passively receiving information.

### 2. Cultural Continuity
Emphasizes the continuity of Huaxia civilization while respecting cultural differences across regions.

### 3. Openness and Freedom
Fully open source, allows free forking and commercial use.

### 4. Privacy First
Supports local AI deployment, data stored on users' own servers.
